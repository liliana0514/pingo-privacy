## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need an account to use Pingo?**
No. Pingo creates an anonymous profile for you automatically when you first open the app. You join and create scheduling rooms using a PIN code — no email or sign-up required.

**How do I join a room?**
Ask whoever created the room for the 4-digit PIN, then enter it on Pingo's home screen under "got a PIN?"

**How do I delete my data?**
Open Pingo, go to **Settings → Delete My Data**. This permanently deletes your account, display name, time zone, and all availability you've submitted. This cannot be undone.

**Why isn't the widget updating?**
The Pingo widget updates when the Pingo app runs in the background or is opened. If a room's status changed (like a time being confirmed) while your device was idle, opening the app will refresh the widget.
