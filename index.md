# Privacy Policy for Pingo

**Effective date:** August 10, 2026

Pingo ("the app," "we," "us") is a group scheduling and availability coordination app. This page explains what information Pingo collects, how it's used, and how you can delete it.

## No account required

Pingo does not require you to sign up, log in, or provide an email address. Each time you install and open Pingo, the app creates an anonymous account for you automatically. You join and create scheduling rooms using a PIN code.

## Information we collect

| Data | Purpose | Linked to you? |
|---|---|---|
| Display name | Shown to other members of a room you're in, so they know who submitted availability | Yes |
| Time zone | Used to align your availability with other members' local times on the scheduling grid | Yes |
| Room membership & availability selections | Core app functionality — lets a room's host see everyone's availability and confirm a time | Yes |

We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or any data unrelated to using Pingo's scheduling features. Pingo does not use analytics SDKs or advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties.

## Home screen widget

Pingo's widget shows your upcoming confirmed events directly on your home screen. This data is stored only in a local, on-device App Group container shared between the Pingo app and its widget — it is not transmitted anywhere beyond the normal app sync described above.

## Where your data is stored

Pingo's data is stored using [Supabase](https://supabase.com), a hosted PostgreSQL database provider. Data is transmitted securely (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our database.

## How long we keep your data

Scheduling rooms automatically expire and are deleted a set period after creation or after a time is confirmed. When a room is deleted, all related availability and membership data for that room is deleted along with it.

## Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app:

**Settings → Delete My Data**

This immediately and permanently deletes your account, your display name and time zone, your membership in any rooms, and all availability you've submitted. This action cannot be undone.

## Children's privacy

Pingo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above.
